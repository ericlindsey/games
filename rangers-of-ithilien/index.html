        WASD move · Mouse look · Wheel zoom · Shift walk/run

        Space jump · E speak · M map · I pack · Esc free the mouse, menu

        Click or F strike; with the bow, hold to draw and release to loose

        Right button (hold) or R take aim: a shot to the head kills, to the heart wounds deeply

        Q sword / bow · H eat or drink to heal
      

      

        Left thumb moves. Drag on the right to look; pinch to zoom.

        Attack picks the nearest enemy: an arrow if they are far, the sword if they are near.

        Aim lets you choose the mark yourself: steer with the left thumb or a drag, then Attack to loose.
        A shot to the head kills; to the heart wounds deeply.

        To play without the browser's bars, add the game to your home screen
        (in Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.
        Its save is kept apart from the browser's.
